# Human Made Money — Intellectual Property Strategy

## Purpose

This working strategy identifies potentially valuable intellectual property without making legal conclusions. Counsel should review ownership, patentability, registrability, licensing, and jurisdiction-specific steps before reliance.

## Core assets

### Software and documentation

Protect source code, tests, schemas, interfaces, product copy, visual assets, internal documentation, and operational tooling through clear ownership, contributor agreements, access controls, and license tracking.

### Potentially distinctive technology

- Agreement modeling with versioned multi-party acceptance.
- Evidence provenance and explainable assessment against accepted conditions.
- Conditional funding intent that remains distinct from reserved funds.
- Policy-controlled resolution and settlement workflows with human review.
- Scoped AI/MCP orchestration with source attribution, least privilege, and auditability.

Describe these as integrated trust-layer capabilities, not as autonomous AI adjudication or escrow as the entire business. A patentability assessment must compare specific claims with prior art; broad product ideas alone are not protectable inventions.

### Trade secrets

Candidates include non-public risk rules, evaluation datasets, prompt/configuration methods, fraud signals, reconciliation procedures, operational playbooks, security architecture, and roadmap research. Maintain secrecy through least privilege, confidentiality terms, repository controls, vendor review, logging, and offboarding.

### Brand

Evaluate protection for Human Made Money, HMM, product names, logos, and distinctive marks. Conduct clearance searches before use or filing. “Challenge” and “escrow” are descriptive product terms and should not define the brand architecture.

## Protection principles

- Establish written ownership for founders, employees, and contractors.
- Inventory third-party and open-source licenses.
- Preserve dated invention records and relevant prototypes.
- Separate public marketing claims from confidential implementation detail.
- Do not publish unsupported claims of patent, legal enforceability, custody, or autonomous settlement.
- Review this strategy with qualified counsel at material product or jurisdiction changes.
