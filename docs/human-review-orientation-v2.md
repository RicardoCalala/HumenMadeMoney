<!-- BEGIN V2 ORIENTATION -->
This service helps people make clear agreements, share information about what happened, and reach an outcome through stated steps.

A computer-generated assessment may compare the agreement with the information provided. It may point out what supports a conclusion, what is missing or uncertain, and suggest what could happen next. This is advice. A recommendation, explanation, or confidence level does not give the computer permission to decide or act.

The computer cannot decide who wins, approve a final outcome, appoint or replace a required person, give someone permission to act, clear a required safety check, or make a binding legal or financial choice. It cannot hold, charge, send, release, refund, or otherwise move money.

Consequential action needs the separate checks and permissions that apply to the agreement. Depending on the situation, those may include a participant's choice, a person's review, fixed authorization rules, a challenge or review period, and financial safety checks. A computer recommendation does not bypass any of them.

Any money-related features discussed in this validation are simulated. No real money is held or moved.
<!-- END V2 ORIENTATION -->
