# Human Made Money — Product Experience

## Purpose

This document translates the Product Bible into a coherent product journey. The Agreement is the primary object. Evidence, verification, protection, intent, settlement, and reputation support an agreement; none defines the whole product.

## Experience principles

- Start with the commitment: purpose, people, obligations, timing, evidence, and resolution.
- Make state, ownership, next action, and consequences understandable to every participant.
- Use escrow only when money needs protection; non-financial and intent-based agreements remain first-class.
- Present AI output as sourced, explainable assistance. Participants retain authority over contested or consequential outcomes.
- Treat uncertainty, amendment, cancellation, expiry, dispute, and recovery as designed paths.

## Primary journey

### 1. Discover and onboard

Explain that Human Made Money helps people create and complete trustworthy agreements. Let users explore before verification, collect only necessary identity data, and distinguish account creation from identity checks required by a later capability.

### 2. Create an agreement

The guided builder captures:

1. purpose and plain-language summary;
2. participants, roles, and permissions;
3. obligations, milestones, deadlines, and success conditions;
4. evidence requirements and approved sources;
5. funding mode: none, optional protection, or conditional intent;
6. verification policy, confidence expectations, and human-review route;
7. cancellation, expiry, dispute, and resolution rules.

Drafts remain reversible. AI may identify ambiguity and suggest clearer language, but must show proposed changes and never silently rewrite intent.

### 3. Review and accept

Invitees see the same version, their responsibilities, evidence rules, financial exposure when relevant, and the resolution path. Material amendments create a new version and reset affected acceptance. Acceptance is explicit and attributable.

### 4. Activate or protect

Non-funded agreements can activate after required acceptance. If protection is selected, show amount, currency, fees, custody status, release/refund conditions, and failure handling before confirmation. A funding intent is a conditional commitment, not reserved or guaranteed money.

### 5. Monitor and submit evidence

The agreement home shows purpose, lifecycle state, next action, participants, deadlines, protection status when relevant, and recent activity. Evidence records source, submitter, timestamp, integrity context, and visibility. Retrieved facts, participant claims, and AI inferences stay visually distinct.

### 6. Verify and review

The system compares evidence with accepted terms and returns an assessment containing sources, matched conditions, gaps, confidence, and a recommended next action. Participants can approve, challenge, add evidence, or request review. Low-confidence, conflicting, or high-impact cases require human review.

### 7. Resolve and close

Only an authorized actor or explicit approved policy may trigger a consequential transition. The interface confirms the exact outcome before recording completion, cancellation, expiry, refund, release, or another declared resolution. AI never directly releases funds or makes an irreversible legal or financial decision.

## Core surfaces

- **Home:** urgent actions, active agreements, recent events, and understandable status.
- **My Agreements:** drafts, invitations, active work, review, disputes, and closed records.
- **Agreement detail:** Overview, Terms, Participants, Evidence, Protection, Activity, and Resolution.
- **Protection:** funding status and transaction history where implemented.
- **Verification:** assessments, evidence gaps, and human-review queues.
- **Organizations:** templates, roles, policies, integrations, and reporting.

## Trust and reputation

Reputation, if implemented, should use attributable behaviors such as accepted obligations completed, timely responses, and resolved disputes. Never reduce trust to an unexplained score or let an AI assessment become a permanent verdict. Provide correction and appeal paths.

## Success and future scope

Measure successfully resolved agreements with informed participant consent, comprehension, evidence sufficiency, resolution time, dispute/appeal rates, accessibility, and safety. Future organization, developer, and authorized-agent workflows must preserve versioned consent, least privilege, auditability, and human oversight.
