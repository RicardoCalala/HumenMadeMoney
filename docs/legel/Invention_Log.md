# Human Made Money — Invention Log

## Purpose

This log records concepts and evidence of development. It is not a patentability opinion or a substitute for dated source history, inventor confirmation, assignment documents, or legal advice.

## Entry: Dynamic intent funding

**Status:** Concept under development
**Date first recorded:** See repository history and supporting records.

### Description

A participant may record a conditional intent to fund an agreement when declared conditions occur. The system distinguishes intent from authorized, reserved, or transferred funds and handles scope, expiry, notification, confirmation, provider failure, and audit history.

### Related systems

Agreement Engine, Intent Monitor, Evidence and Verification, Protection, Notifications, and Audit History.

### Open invention questions

Document the specific novel mechanism, inventors, prototypes, prior-art search, failure handling, and jurisdictional constraints. Avoid representing an intent as guaranteed money.

## Entry: Explainable agreement assessment

**Status:** Concept under development
**Date first recorded:** See repository history and supporting records.

### Description

A bounded AI-assisted workflow structures an agreement, identifies permitted evidence sources, preserves provenance, compares evidence with the exact accepted version, reports supporting and conflicting facts, expresses uncertainty, and routes the result to participants or human review.

### Related systems

Agreement Engine, Evidence Store, Verification Service, scoped MCP tools, Policy Engine, Human Review, and Audit History.

### Authority boundary

The AI output is an assessment and recommendation. It does not autonomously adjudicate a dispute, change accepted terms, release funds, or execute settlement. Any potentially protectable implementation should be described in terms of its concrete controls and data flow, not a broad claim over AI resolution.

## Recordkeeping

For each future entry, record title, contributors/inventors, dates, problem, concrete mechanism, diagrams or prototypes, alternatives considered, public disclosures, related commits, and counsel review. Keep confidential material access-controlled.
