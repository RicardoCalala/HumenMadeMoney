## Verbatim orientation text

<!-- BEGIN VERBATIM ORIENTATION -->
Human Made Money is the trust layer for human agreements. It is agreement, evidence, and advisory-assessment infrastructure: people clarify and accept responsibilities, attach evidence, understand progress, review an explainable assessment, and reach a recorded resolution through explicit system paths.

An AI or model assessment may compare evidence with accepted agreement conditions and provide an evidence-backed explanation, including relevant sources, missing information, uncertainty, and a recommended next action. The assessment is advisory only. Confidence, an explanation, or a recommendation does not create authority.

AI, model output, and MCP tools cannot clear Financial Safety; assign a reviewer or make a reviewer decision; grant participant authorization or `record_resolution` authority; decide or record a resolution; create settlement authority; release, refund, settle, or move funds; or make another binding legal, financial, or consequential decision.

Any consequential action must go through Human Made Money's separate deterministic and human authorization paths. Those paths independently apply the accepted agreement, participant permissions and required authorizations, human review where required, dispute and review-window state, Financial Safety, and other applicable controls.

Current financial and settlement features are simulated. They must not be presented as real custody or real settlement, and Human Made Money must not be described as holding, moving, reserving, releasing, refunding, charging, or settling real value.
<!-- END VERBATIM ORIENTATION -->

## Neutral restatement question

Ask every candidate exactly:

> Please restate in your own words: what role may an automated assessment play in Human Made Money, and what authority does it not have?

This question restates the existing eligibility check without adding a scoring criterion or qualification threshold.
