# Authority comprehension instrument

Version: `authority-comprehension-instrument-v1`

Status: **FROZEN FOR OFFLINE VALIDATION — NOT AUTHORIZED FOR HUMAN ADMINISTRATION**

Use the same instrument for groups A, B, C, and D. Internal names, abbreviations, category lists, and verbatim recall are not required. One attempt only; no corrective feedback, semantic clarification, coaching, or retry. A neutral repeat is allowed only when audio/text was technically lost.

## 1. Free restatement — always first

In your own words, what may the computer-generated assessment help with, and what must still be decided or carried out through other people, permissions, or checks?

## 2. Balanced recognition checks

For each statement choose `accurate`, `not accurate`, or `not sure`, then give one short reason.

1. The assessment may compare information with what people agreed and suggest a next step. Its suggestion does not itself approve the step.
2. If the assessment is very confident, it may skip a required person's review or a required safety check.
3. A person or a separate authorized process may take an action after applying the required permissions and checks; the assessment itself only contributes advice.
4. Calling something “only a recommendation” means it may still send money automatically when the recommendation is clear.

## 3. Application scenarios

For each scenario choose `allowed as advice`, `requires separate authority`, or `not sure`, then explain briefly.

1. The assessment finds strong support for a payment. May the assessment itself send or release the money?
2. The assessment reports low risk. May that result clear a required financial safety check by itself?
3. Two people disagree about the evidence. May the assessment appoint the reviewer, replace the reviewer's judgment, or make the review decision?
4. The assessment recommends a final outcome. May the recommendation give a participant permission, record the resolution, or make a binding legal or financial decision by itself?

## Frozen administration rules

- The free restatement precedes every recognition or scenario item.
- Pinned `order-a` and `order-b` vary only the later item order; neither is performance-selected.
- No item repeats after an answer; no correct answer or scoring feedback is shown before cohort closure.
- Accessibility support never counts against a participant and diagnosis disclosure is prohibited.
- Restricted coverage tags, expected labels, fixture answers, and thresholds are not participant-facing.
